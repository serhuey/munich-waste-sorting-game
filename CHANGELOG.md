# Changelog

What changed for a player, release by release. How the numbers are chosen:
[`docs/RELEASING.md`](docs/RELEASING.md).

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-24

The first numbered release, and the starting point of the count. It is the
build on the internal test at `sergei-grieg.de/muell/`.

- Five places on a ladder — home, the shop, the Wertstoffinsel, the
  Wertstoffhof, hazardous drop-off — and 106 objects, every one traced to an
  AWM source and signed by a person.
- Objects to examine (the answer is printed on them) and objects to take apart,
  with an explanation after every answer.
- The Christmas-tree finale: strip the tree, choose a route on two dates.
- Both sides of 1 January 2027: the yellow bin appears at home and the
  packaging containers leave the island on that day, in the same build, with a
  notice through the transition.
- German and English.
- The result as a picture card to share; the address on it is the one the game
  was opened at.
- Installable as an app, with its own icon; the installed app plays offline.
- The release number on the title screen.
- Impressum and Datenschutz from every screen. No cookies, no statistics.
